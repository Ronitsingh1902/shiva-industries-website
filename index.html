About Shiva Industries

A journey built on hard work, trust & determination.

Every successful business begins with a dream. For Shiva Industries, that dream began in 2001, when our founder, Mr. Gulab Chand Natani, came to Delhi determined to build a better future through hard work and dedication.

He began in the steel utensils manufacturing industry with a first salary of just ₹2,500. Over the next 15 years, he gained hands-on experience and rose to the position of Senior Accountant, a role he held until the end of his tenure in 2016. Those years shaped his understanding of manufacturing, financial discipline, and lasting customer relationships.

In 2016–17, he turned his dream into reality by establishing Shiva Industries. The business navigated the uncertainty of the COVID-19 pandemic with patience and resilience, emerging with renewed determination to serve customers reliably.
